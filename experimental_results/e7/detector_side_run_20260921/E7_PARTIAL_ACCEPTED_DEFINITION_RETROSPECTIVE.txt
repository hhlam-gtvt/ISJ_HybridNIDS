E7 RETROSPECTIVE REVIEW-OUTCOME DEFINITION

Status:
  Post-run documentation amendment.
  This definition was NOT preregistered before E7_CUONG_FULL_20260921T101315Z
  and must not be represented as a historically frozen rule.

ACCEPTED
  The sampled incident can be traced to the declared source session and the
  evidence required for its recorded provenance tier is internally consistent.
  No material discrepancy is found in incident identity, source linkage,
  timing basis, or detector evidence used by that tier.

PARTIAL_ACCEPTED
  The incident identity and source session are traceable and at least one
  material evidence path is verifiable, but one or more fields/evidence links
  required to fully validate the recorded incident are missing, unavailable,
  or materially incomplete. The discrepancy does not overturn the incident
  identity, but it prevents full acceptance. Such a record may support
  continuity/provenance observations only and must not be upgraded into a
  stronger detector-correctness or autonomous-response claim.

REJECTED
  A material mismatch exists in incident identity, source-session linkage,
  provenance tier, timing/correlation basis, or detector evidence such that
  the recorded incident cannot be supported by the preserved evidence.

Important:
  Absence of a Suricata alert is not by itself grounds for PARTIAL_ACCEPTED
  when the recorded provenance tier is RF_ONLY_REVIEW. Review is against the
  evidence contract of the tier actually recorded.
